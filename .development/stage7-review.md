This supports local instruction selection under larger record sets and changed dependency wiring; it does not demonstrate language parsing, graph traversal, arithmetic execution, or full compositional evaluation. The generator has a sharp latent probability transition at .9985. This controlled supplied-score experiment cannot certify calibrated confidence from learned proposals. Exact schema validity is supplied, and the factor product is a score. Thresholds are chosen on validation, frozen for test, with matched local/global/product threshold selection. Report precision, executable recall, every denominator, empirical factor correlations, reliability, and mixed-group behavior. Autonomous candidate coupling requires both actual proposal competence and separately validated proposal-derived confidence; the present B diagnostic alone does not supply the latter.

C1 supervises workspace reconstruction of perfect typed returns. Exact external-register integrity is architectural and separate from learned workspace value/type/primitive/identity recovery. Release stops record availability without erasing the workspace; stale latent information remains possible. The task and validity heads are untrained diagnostics in C1. Late-use training and multi-operation composition remain blocked until C competence. Random unrelated distractors test interference, not preservation of a separately queried unrelated fact.

D receives supplied certified typed evidence and persistent current snapshots, with an explicit public deadline. Learned stop uses its own logits, not the core timing bias. Role, certification, and queried-name marginal counts remain constant across arrival; their conjunction identifies usable evidence. No-solution terminates at the supplied deadline and must predict reject. Test is evaluated only after a frozen final main budget. Passing this independent diagnostic does not authorize runtime composition.

E deduplicates complete alpha-normalized ordered graph strings before splitting. Repeated identity, values, edge roles and slots are preserved. Its decoder metric compares full canonical structure under the pinned compiler traversal, not arbitrary isomorphism or denotational semantics. Values use a train-only vocabulary and unknown values fail exact semantics. Visible-token pointers canonicalize repeated spellings. The fixed evaluation prefix and subsequent nested training prefix keep evaluation constructions stable and disjoint across cardinality budgets, provided data seed, difficulty and evaluation count remain fixed. Actual visited graphs and optimizer/token exposures must accompany nominal cardinality. Gate F stays false absent paired learning trends above baselines and renderer/lexicon transfer; presence alone cannot pass.

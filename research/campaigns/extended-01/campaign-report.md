# Campaign scientific report

The campaign establishes replicated known-motif graph acquisition, restricted learned use of returned scalars, and functioning bounded composition. It does not establish omitted-combination transfer, uniform numerical reliability, autonomous scheduling or a unique programmable-attention advantage. Strong alternatives match the attention and composition successes. Broader semantic exposure and a privileged correct-node intervention leave the omitted combination unsolved.

The campaign closes because these branches have reached meaningful evidential convergence, not because the compute ceiling was exhausted. No further model jobs follow from this report. Historical failures remain immutable; new scoped contracts do not replace them. The [claim map](claim-map.md), [evidence index](evidence-index.md) and [figure guide](figures/README.md) link the complete record.

## Returns and composition: useful, restricted interfaces

[R04](returns/R04-report.md) passes its registered original-mixture scalar contract across three historical backbones. Equal optimizer presentations make the smaller-pool readout a meaningful comparison. Balanced diagnostics nevertheless expose serious subtype/value failures, including an entire float-zero stratum. Historical backbones informed development; this is not a fresh architecture-level replication or numerical-range generalization. [Population audit](review/R04-population-audit.json), [balanced strata](review/R04-balanced-strata.json).

[R05](returns/R05-confirmation-report.md) confirms downstream decision use and wrong/drop/swap dependence on the returned fact. Its binary answer can stay correct despite scalar error, particularly away from decision boundaries. Finite-domain decision success therefore does not restore exact numerical information. Exact-value neural and query-only baselines expose both ceiling and public prior. [Audit summary](review/R05-confirmation-summary.json), [curves](figures/returns-composition.svg).

[R10/R11](returns/R10-R11-closeout.md) close the local tail search without an attested repair. Larger diversity and lower LR improve some outcomes, but both fixed calibration screens fail. Reused validation cannot replace the gate. R11 isolates decay versus constant LR from the same recovered optimizer fork; the inherited endpoint is not an equal-additional-exposure control. [Audits](review/R11-development-summary-audit.json), [development curves](figures/return-tail-development.svg).

[C04](composition/C04-confirmation-report.md) passes across three independently audited lineages, but so do the primary fixed-4000 role-trained neural and supplied single-return-copy alternatives. Selected checkpoints remain secondary. Finite numerical overlap and supplied scheduling preclude new-arithmetic or autonomous-orchestration claims. Workspace delay-16 execution is roughly 47–50 times slower in the recorded batch-one comparison; that timing excludes setup/training. [Final audit](review/C04-final-aggregate-audit.json), [accuracy and timing](figures/composition-accuracy-timing.svg).

## Attention: engineered policy improvement, strong baseline ties

[A14](attention/a14-report.md) changes hardest-condition task counts from 176/187/177 to 1019/1007/1015 of 1024 across three seeds by using one common model-selected destination for value heads. This is a supplied inference rule acting on learned scores. Complete queried routes, task answers and all-node suffixes are distinct outcomes.

All nine A06 engineering references achieve perfect observed task and suffix scores on the same fresh graphs. Their training histories and interfaces differ; parameter/FLOP matching and architectural superiority are not established. The learned-soft failure remains visible. [Final audit](review/A14-final-aggregate-audit.json), [confirmation figure](figures/attention-confirmation.svg).

## Semantics: acquisition replicates; omitted transfer does not

[S12](semantics/S12-report.md) confirms directional LR-decay improvement: calibrated complete graphs rise from zero to 66/131/139 of 1024, with raw counts 2/6/11. Seed 701 misses competence, so directional gain is not an all-seed pass. [Audit](review/S12-final-aggregate-audit.json), [paired figure](figures/semantic-confirmation.svg).

The subsequent development and frozen diagnostic sequence preserves negative results: [S13](semantics/S13-report.md) learns Spanish components without complete Spanish graphs and sacrifices English recovery; [S14](semantics/S14-report.md) fails unseen motifs; [S15](semantics/S15-report.md) fails acquisition/retention/recombination. [S16](semantics/S16-results.md) programs spelling invariance while reducing original accuracy. [S17](semantics/S17-report.md) shows a calibration contribution without retroactively passing S15. [S18](semantics/S18-report.md) improves some components but fails every promotion gate, including on optimistic calibration-overlapping TRAIN diagnostics. These outcomes show partial learning, not absence of all information. Curves and independent receipts are indexed in the [evidence guide](evidence-index.md).

[S19](semantics/S19-report.md) first demonstrates strong known-motif acquisition with a scratch sequential learner on one inspected development seed. [S20](semantics/S20-report.md) then confirms the fixed learner on fresh constructions: known-motif macro accuracy is 95.898%, 94.727% and 94.857% across seeds 701/702/703, mean 95.161%. Every seed passes macro/cell competence and paired superiority against both original and contextual matched-calibrated baselines. All raw comparisons remain visible. Every arm and seed scores zero on held-out 3×4. [Final audit](review/S20-final-confirmation-audit.json), [endpoints](figures/semantic-confirmation-shapes.svg), [paired effects](figures/semantic-confirmation-differences.svg).

S20 matches 32,768 incremental presentations. Workspace learners inherit 196,608 presentations and have 57,853,781 parameters; record starts from scratch with 62,677,315. Objectives and arithmetic differ. This confirms the learner comparison, not an isolated decoder-architecture cause. Shared-event intervals condition on the fixed models; displayed seed variation is not a seed-population confidence interval.

[S21](semantics/S21-report.md) uses the same scratch initialization and learner to compare three- versus six-motif corpora. New-motif acquisition and old-known retention pass, but omitted 3×4 remains zero: the joint gate fails and conditional confirmation is ineligible. Equal presentations do not match token/record work. A [separate strict audit](review/S21-isomorphism-result-audit.json) rules out node renumbering for all 798 valid held-out outputs through joint node-attribute multiset mismatch; 226 invalid outputs remain invalid. This conclusion does not follow from canonical-position errors alone. [Primary audit](review/S21-final-development-audit.json), [learning](figures/semantic-broad-motif-learning.svg), [endpoints](figures/semantic-broad-motif-endpoints.svg).

[S22](semantics/S22-report.md) supplies progressively stronger node information to both frozen S21 checkpoints. Every held-out condition remains zero for exact relations and complete graphs, including full correct node prefixes. Nodes are supplied correctly on all 512 prefix events; strict scored exact-node counts of 421/311 additionally require valid final output. Partial relation overlap survives. This privileged diagnostic establishes that correcting this early interface alone is insufficient for these models; it is neither learned promotion nor a universal capacity theorem. [Final audit](review/S22-final-diagnostic-audit.json), [oracle-boundary figure](figures/semantic-oracle-boundary.svg).

## Closure and remaining limits

Confirmation and exploration remain distinct: S20 is fresh three-lineage confirmation; S21 is one paired development intervention; S22 is a frozen oracle diagnostic. Repeated policies, delays and shared events do not multiply independent support. Conditional empirical zero-difference intervals do not prove population equivalence.

The frozen [budget ledger](budget.json) records 31,061.728823924484 charged seconds (8.628258 hours) across 119 ledger jobs, including one failed job; ledger entries are not literal OS-process counts. The [closeout decision](closeout-decision.md) retains 12,138.271 seconds of the 12-hour allocation unused. No GPU jobs remain. Unused capacity is not a reason to launch additional work. The ineligible S21 confirmation stays closed; no exposure/calibration sweep or presumed node-only learned repair is promoted. A future study would need a separately registered, discriminating question about relation acquisition versus structural combination. This campaign identifies that boundary, not a demonstrated remedy.

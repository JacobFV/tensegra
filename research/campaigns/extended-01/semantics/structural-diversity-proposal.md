# Prospective controlled structural acquisition branch

Design only, pending completion of the fixed S13 pair and separate approval. No new constructions, model runs, source mutation, budget, or promotion are authorized by this note. Preserve S14 as the original inspected zero-shot diagnostic.

One useful next comparison is **English-only arity exposure with a held-out arity × fact-count combination**. The pinned generator varies global predicate arity and three versus four facts. It does not generate mixed local arities, alternative branch placement, or deeper trees; difficulty changes arity, not depth. Consequently this proposal tests a narrow structural recombination and must not be described as broad shape transfer.

Both arms start from the same fixed S11 dev201 model and AdamW state, with the same actor, LR 1e-5, renderer, unique-graph count, batch size, and update/exposure budget. Subject to a bounded support audit, use 8192 unique English training constructions per arm:

- Control: 8192 arity-four constructions, balanced across three and four facts (two ordered motifs).
- Diversity: 4096 shared arity-four constructions, balanced across fact counts, plus 4096 arity-three/three-fact constructions (three ordered motifs total).

The held-out combination is arity-three/four-fact: one ordered motif absent from both continuation training sets and the fixed parent. The diversity arm has seen arity three and has separately seen four-fact lists, while all argument and list-slot labels remain within historical support. Evaluation separates fresh arity-three/three-fact acquisition, fresh arity-four retention, and the held-out arity-three/four-fact combination. Training arity three makes its three-fact test acquisition, not transfer. The fourth combination was already observed in S14, so this is an explicitly S14-informed hypothesis with new construction-level confirmation, not an uninspected motif discovery claim.

Before freezing, independently audit attainable unique alpha-distinct counts for each cell, exact ordered motif counts, construction non-overlap with all old TRAIN/DEV/confirmation, S13 and S14, capacity, identity, types, edge order, copy support, lexical support, and English textual identifiability. The previous 1024-example pooled arity-three population does not prove 4096 unique examples in its three-fact cell. If support saturates, report the restricted population and redesign the matched counts before any run; never silently duplicate examples or change the generator. Use independently named development and confirmation namespaces and freeze confirmation before inspecting new development outcomes.

Keep the generator source unchanged; version the cache selection/split builder independently. Use common-example schedule slots and matched total presentations. Different graph shapes change negative-pair draw counts: a future trainer must either isolate negative-sampling RNG by presentation so changed examples cannot perturb later common-example draws, or explicitly report that pairing limitation. Do not claim identical negative pairs for different graphs. Retain the original English TRAIN calibration population and fixed calibration procedure, applying each endpoint's one policy to every evaluation cell without per-arity fitting.

A first several-thousand-update development tranche (for example the existing 4096 × 8 exposure scale) is reasonable only after data/source audit, timing profile, and root allocation. Unique counts and presentations match, but graph/token sizes differ; record processed nodes, edges, tokens, and wall time as exposure covariates. The intervention jointly adds a motif and redistributes exposure, so its estimand is the benefit of this controlled training mixture, not a universal diversity effect.

If trained arity-three acquisition improves but the held-out combination fails, that distinguishes acquisition from even this narrow recombination. If acquisition itself remains poor, keep the S09 contextual-text alternative in consideration. Local-arity/branch/depth variation would require a separately versioned generator and independent semantics/rendering audit; no such extension is proposed for implementation now.

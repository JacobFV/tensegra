Earlier generator-only support probes were not learned/validation/confirmation populations and did not reserve examples; they are not added as though they were experiment datasets.

## Builder interface

`research/results/campaign-01/semantics/s21-prior-alpha-exclusions.json.gz` is a gzipped JSON sorted list of hash strings. Its SHA256 is `8c707067b1248095be8b2d426da7c930d4e650c9866008cf262b032cac8d9349`. Bind this and the adjacent `s21-exclusion-inventory.json` metadata hash in the builder config. The metadata enumerates all cache hashes, counts, regeneration checks and caveats.

Fresh S21 rows must avoid this union. The prospectively specified reused S15 TRAIN subsets and reused DEV rows are explicit exceptions, verified against their exact source records; they are not fresh constructions. New TRAIN/DEV/confirmation must also be mutually alpha-disjoint. Conservative exclusion does not change generator semantics, but may reduce available support; preserve a shortfall rather than silently loosening exclusions.

Current-phase occupancy is not total acquisition cost. The hybrid inherits several trained interfaces; N1 shares its P01 initialization and adds new end-to-end fitting, while N2 starts its contextual model from scratch. No equal-total-compute or speedup claim follows from equal current baseline presentations. Earlier development/search costs also exist beyond the selected checkpoint lineage below.

The C01 exact-copy and query-only controls additionally reuse their separately trained R05 heads; they do not belong to the main learned-return path. Backbone examples, recurrent microsteps, cached readout rows and consumer rows are different units. Summing them into one apparently matched training-example budget would conceal different work. Frozen feature extraction and all evaluations still consume process time, reported separately by phase receipts.

For C01 development, each N1/N2 arm prospectively runs two learning rates×4000updates×256=2048000 current optimizer presentations. Calibration selects LR/endpoint; the selected checkpoint may embody fewer updates, but full search occupancy and all sample visitation remain recorded. N1 begins with the same P01303 selected state as the hybrid and may fine-tune it; N2 does not inherit P01. The optional N1-frozen diagnostic is not scheduled. The hybrid's current development phase performs zero optimization and consumes117.989399920s of execution/evaluation/export. This is not evidence that the hybrid required less total training compute.

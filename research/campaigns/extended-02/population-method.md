# Synchronous population process

The versioned runner supports **PBT**, **independent multistart**, and **one conventional learner**. Every round grants six update slots and six evaluations on exactly the same declared development episodes. PBT and multistart grant one slot to each member. Single grants all six slots sequentially to initial-bank member zero, evaluating after each tranche; only its latest final-round checkpoint is eligible. Finalists are maximum utility among the **current** final population, with slot-index tie breaking. No confirmation data is read. Initial bank seeds are identical across procedures, although single uses only the first bank member. Equal update/evaluation allocations do not assert equal compute, and family comparisons must report actual work.

PBT completes all six evaluations, ranks verified utility, and replaces the two worst members with the two best when donor utility is strictly greater. Equal-score populations do not replace. Compatible weights and either AdamW state or explicitly reset optimizer state transfer. Learning-rate and entropy coefficients independently mutate by 0.8/1.2, bounded to [1e-6,1e-2] and [1e-6,1]. Mutation records retain parents, checkpoint hashes, preselection scores, optimizer policy, and generation. No replacement follows the final round because such untrained copies cannot improve its finalist. Descendants share ancestry, not independent-seed status.

Recipient data cursors, cumulative allocated updates, presentations, episodes, data-hash chain, and RNG are retained after replacement. This prevents child replay of donor data or falsified allocated-work counts. Inherited weights have additional ancestral exposure; ancestry checkpoints preserve that history. Each slot has its own disjoint seed interval. Single consumes a longer contiguous interval; equal counts do not imply identical literal training episodes, but all procedures use the same deterministic public world mixture. A world seed selects its mixture component by a separate hash and opaque record addresses through another stream. No evaluator state controls mixture assignment.

Training methods may be prespecified per round (supervised bootstrap or actor-critic). The public teacher is explicitly privileged training supervision and sees only the declared actor API. A frozen protocol includes public world kwargs, training allocations, initialization seeds, and development support. Source hashes must match for resume. New recipes require new output roots.

Each completed slot has immutable weights, optimizer/RNG state, compact raw development trajectories, seed intervals, training curves, and process timings. The atomic coordinator state retains pending slots and mutation RNG. A hard-interrupted attempt is preserved and its missing inner duration explicitly delegated to the outer resource ledger; it is not assigned zero cost. Model and optimizer tensors are moved to CPU when idle. Solver-worker CPU is present in raw episode outcomes; parent CPU, reaped-child CPU, solver CPU, and GPU occupancy are distinct quantities and must not be blindly summed twice. The outer coordinator records complete process occupancy and limits.

The CLI accepts only `--config` and `--output`, resumes that exact protocol, and opens a bounded solver context. It contains no campaign launch decision, autonomous host access, adaptive curriculum, or confirmation selection. Root must profile and approve budget allocation within the user's authorization before main runs. Mechanical tests use fake small checkpoints only; main policy width defaults to1024.

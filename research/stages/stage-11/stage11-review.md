# Stage 11 independent review

Baseline: `e10b80a`. This audit owns no training jobs. Historical files and gate outcomes remain immutable; the existing ID-separated belief interface is frozen, with no further belief training.

## Preregistered review constraints

Standard width remains 1024. Total GPU ceiling is 180 minutes, allocated 75 minutes to returns, 75 to semantic acquisition, and 30 reserve. Profiles must precede fixed main budgets. Main comparisons use three paired initialization seeds and at least 512 examples per generalization cell. Small fixed-set fitting is an acquisition diagnostic, not a generalization result.

No architecture expansion, structural-attention intervention, autonomous composition, or supervision withdrawal is authorized. These supporting interface gates do not universally gate the original programmable-attention hypothesis.

## Return comparison

Compare the unchanged short-delay backbone/readout recipe (training through four updates) against the same architecture trained through sixteen updates. Explicitly match or report optimizer presentations, recurrent work, delay sampling, memory allocation, scalar readout fitting exposure, and checkpoint selection. Changing recurrent length changes compute even at equal example counts.

Delay32 must remain entirely absent from training and calibration for both backbone and pooled readout. Frozen final evaluation alone may inspect it; per-delay diagnostic heads trained at32 cannot be treated as extrapolation. Include delay0 and every covered delay, all required fields, and the inherited exact retention gate rather than scalar means alone. Pair events across delays, conditions and arms; independent seeds are not new test constructions. Readout improvements and backbone improvements require distinct attribution.

## Public-text semantic acquisition

The passed privileged fixed-node gate permits a separate public-text acquisition investigation. It supplies no proof that surface understanding will work. First predeclare a small complete-graph fitting gate and budget, with public input identifiability, node/copy/slot capacity and semantic diversity audited. Train-only thresholds may be fitted by a frozen rule; inference uses predicted edges. Gold nodes/edges may supervise losses but cannot become actor inputs.

Fresh known-renderer construction evaluation proceeds only after fixed-set complete fitting succeeds. A heldout renderer or motif stage proceeds only after its prerequisite gate. Every comparison must retain raw versus calibrated graph recovery and component metrics. Missing families, duplicated alpha-equivalent graphs, or tiny finite construction support cannot silently count as512 independent heldout graphs. No broadened language or semantic-transfer claim from fitted training graphs.

## Independent verification plan

Review source/protocol selection boundaries before main launch. Reconstruct raw counts and gates without importing producer metrics; inspect pairing, data/hash provenance and checkpoint identity. For retained feature/score caches, replay frozen decoding on CPU where practical. Distinguish numerical reconstruction from fresh inference. Recheck all baseline tracked bytes at completion, review report claims against raw evidence, and preserve failed attempts and stopping decisions.
